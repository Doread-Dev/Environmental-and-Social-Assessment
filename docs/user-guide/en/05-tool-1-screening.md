# Tool 1 – Screening

The screening is the first look at a project: which risk category it belongs to, and which
negative and positive impacts it may have. It is filled in and submitted by the project team, and
approved or rejected by a reviewer. Tool 2 opens only when the screening is approved.

Open it from the project's left menu: **Screening – Tool 1**. The page is called
**Environmental Integration Screening**.

<a id="screening-fill-in" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Fill in the screening

The form has three parts.

### **1. Screening Information**

- **Program Officer Name** and **Program Officer Position**: filled in by ESMS with your name and
  job title (or role). You cannot type here. When the screening is submitted, the person who
  submits it is recorded as the officer.
- **Screening Date**: today's date to start with. Change it if the screening was done on another
  day. Required.

### **2. Project Risk Category**

Choose one category:

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A – **High Risk** | significant environmental or social impacts; a full impact assessment is needed |
| B – **Low-Moderate Risk** | low to moderate risks that can be mitigated |
| C – **Negligible Risk** | minimal or no impacts |
| D – **Emergency** | an emergency response project with a faster process |
| E – **Insufficient Info** | more information must be gathered |
| F – **Positive Impact** | a net positive environmental or social impact |

When you choose a category, the box **Category Justification** appears: explain why you chose it.
Required.

### **3. Potential Impacts**

- **Potential Negative Impacts**: possible harm to the environment or people.
- **Potential Positive Impacts**: expected benefits.

Both are required. Write one impact per line: the summary page shows each line as a point of its
own.

<a id="screening-draft" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Save a draft

Press **Save as Draft** at the bottom at any time. Nothing is checked yet: you can save an
unfinished form and come back later. ESMS says **Draft saved successfully**.

If you leave the page with changes that are not saved, ESMS asks first (see
[unsaved changes](11-everyday-situations.md#unsaved-changes)).

<a id="screening-submit" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Submit

1. Press **Submit Screening**. ESMS checks that every required field is filled in and marks what
   is missing.
2. The dialog **Confirm Submission** reminds you that the screening cannot be edited after
   submission unless it is rejected. Press **Confirm Submit**.
3. The summary page opens with the status **Pending Approval**.

From now on the screening is locked for everyone while it waits for the reviewer.

<a id="screening-review" data-requires="approve"></a>
## Review: approve or reject

Open the project's **Screening – Tool 1**. A submitted screening shows its summary: the officer,
the project components, the category with its justification, and the impacts. At the bottom is
**Approval and Recommendations**, with your name in **Approved By** and your **Position**.

### Approve

1. Write your recommendations for the project team in **Recommendations / Next Steps**. They are
   required: the approve button stays disabled while the box is empty.
2. Press **Approve & Complete Screening**.
3. A dialog asks "Approve the screening?". Press **Approve**.

### Reject

1. Press **Reject**. The box **Reason for Rejection** opens.
2. Write why. The reason is optional but strongly recommended: it is what the team reads to
   correct their work.
3. Press **Confirm Rejection**, then **Reject** in the dialog "Reject the screening?".
   **Cancel** closes the box without rejecting.

<a id="screening-after-review"></a>
## After the review

### Approved

- The screening is final. Nobody can change it any more; there is no "reopen".
- Tool 2 (Assessment) opens for the project, whatever the category (A to F, E included).
- The summary shows who approved it and the recommendations. **Proceed to Assessment** takes you
  to Tool 2.
- The project can no longer be deleted, only archived.

### Rejected

- The project's status becomes **Needs Action**.
- The summary shows **Rejected By** and the **Reason for Rejection**.

<a id="screening-resubmit" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Correct a rejected screening

1. Open **Screening – Tool 1** and read the **Reason for Rejection**.
2. Press **Edit Screening**. The form opens with your earlier answers.
3. Correct it, then press **Submit Screening** again. It goes back to the reviewer.

<a id="screening-export" data-requires="export"></a>
## Export

While the screening is submitted or approved, **Export Excel** is in the top bar of the screening
page. It downloads Tool 1 in the AKF layout, with the current status and the date in the file name.
