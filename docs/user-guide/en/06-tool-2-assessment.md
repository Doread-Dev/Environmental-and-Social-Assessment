# Tool 2 – Assessment

The assessment is the site-specific environmental assessment of the project: what the project does
and where, how it was assessed, and a rating of each possible impact. It is filled in and submitted
by the project team, and approved or rejected by a reviewer. Tools 3 and 4 open only when the
assessment is approved.

Open it from the project's left menu: **Assessment – Tool 2**. It has three pages, listed under it
in the menu: **Metadata**, **Methods** and **Scoring**.

While the screening is not approved yet, Tool 2 is locked: the page says **Complete Screening
First** and the menu shows a lock.

<a id="assessment-start" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Start the assessment

The first page, **Environmental Assessment**, shows the project, the workflow progress and a card:

- **Start Environmental Assessment** the first time. ESMS creates the assessment (all fields
  empty, every question at **Negligible**) and opens **Metadata**.
- **Continue Assessment** for an assessment in progress. It opens **Metadata**; move on from there.

<a id="assessment-metadata" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Page 1: Metadata

**Environmental Assessment Metadata**. The top part, **Project & Officer Details**, is filled in by
ESMS: the **Officer Name** and **Officer Position** (yours until someone submits), the screening
**Category** and the project's dates.

Then four questions, each with a text box:

1. **The project (Activity) component being assessed**
2. **Brief description of the project (activities) being carried out**
3. **Briefly describe the environmental setting of the project (activities)**: physical,
   biological and socio-economic characteristics of the site.
4. **The host country's legal requirements related to the environment or natural resources that
   apply to the project and how they will be met**: the relevant laws and how the project will
   comply.

You may leave any of them empty for now: a draft saves as it is. All four are required when you
submit.

Press **Save and Continue**: ESMS saves and opens **Methods**. **Back to Assessment Overview**
goes back to the first page.

<a id="assessment-methods" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Page 2: Methods

**Assessment Methods & Consultation** has two lists. Tick every method that was used; a box for
details opens under each ticked item.

**Environmental Assessment Methods**: **Field visits**, **Previous environmental assessments**,
**Available technical reports**, **Consultation with specialists**, **Project team meetings**,
**AKFS Environmental Guidelines**, **Other methods**.

**Public and Community Consultation**: **Interviews with community members**, **Village or site
meetings**, **Consultation with village/site committees**, **Other community consultation**.

Press **Save and Continue** to save and open **Scoring**. **Back** returns to Metadata.

<a id="assessment-scoring" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Page 3: Scoring

**Environmental Impact Assessment Scoring**: 50 questions in 8 categories. Each category opens and
closes with a click on its title, and shows its score (how many questions are at each level).

For each question (**Assessment Question**):

- **Impact Rating**: **Negligible**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **N/A** (not applicable).
  Every question starts at **Negligible**: change only the ones that are different.
- **Notes / Mitigation**: optional notes or measures.

Below the questions:

- **Total Project Score**: how many questions are at each level.
- **Total Project Impact**: the highest level found (High before Medium before Low before
  Negligible). ESMS works it out; you do not type it.
- **Potential Impacts Summary**: the potential negative and positive impacts of the project as a
  whole. Both are required to submit.

Press **Save as Draft** to save the scores without submitting. The button shows **Saved** when it
is done. **Back** returns to Methods.

<a id="assessment-submit" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Submit

1. On **Scoring**, press **Submit Assessment**.
2. ESMS checks that the four metadata questions and both impact summaries are filled in. If
   something is missing, it says which, for example "Fill in Environmental setting and Legal
   requirements before submitting". Go back to that page, fill it in, save, and submit again.
3. The dialog **Confirm Submission** tells you how many of the 50 questions you changed and that
   the others stay **Negligible**. Check that this is right, then press **Confirm Submit**.
4. The review page opens with the status **Pending Approval**.

From now on the assessment is locked for everyone while it waits for the reviewer.

<a id="assessment-review-page"></a>
## The review page

A submitted, approved or rejected assessment opens on its review page, **Environmental
Assessment** (**Review & Approval Stage**), in **Read Only Mode**: the project and officer, the
four metadata answers, the methods and consultations, every question with its rating (categories
with nothing above Negligible are summed up in one line), the **Overall Assessment Results** and
the impact summaries.

<a id="assessment-review" data-requires="approve"></a>
## Review: approve or reject

At the bottom of the review page, **Approval & Recommendations** shows your name in
**Approved By** and your **Position**.

### Approve

1. Write your conditions, monitoring requirements or recommendations in **Reviewer
   Recommendations** (**(Required for approval)**). The approve button stays disabled while the
   box is empty.
2. Press **Approve & Complete Assessment**.
3. A dialog asks "Approve the assessment?". Press **Approve**.

### Reject

1. Press **Reject**. The box **Reason for Rejection** opens.
2. Write why (optional, but the team needs it to correct the work).
3. Press **Confirm Rejection**, then **Reject** in the dialog "Reject the assessment?".

<a id="assessment-after-review"></a>
## After the review

### Approved

- The assessment is final; nobody can change it any more.
- Tools 3 and 4 open for the project (see [Tools 3 and 4](07-tools-3-4-semp.md)).
- The review page shows the recommendations and **Proceed to SEMP**.

### Rejected

- The project's status becomes **Needs Action**.
- The review page shows **Rejected By** and the reason.

<a id="assessment-resubmit" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Correct a rejected assessment

1. On the review page, read the **Reason for Rejection** and press **Edit Assessment**: it opens
   **Metadata**.
2. Correct what is needed on any of the three pages.
3. Press **Submit Assessment** again on **Scoring**.

<a id="assessment-export" data-requires="export"></a>
## Export

While the assessment is submitted or approved, **Export Excel** is in the top bar of the
assessment pages. It downloads Tool 2 in the AKF layout, with the status and the date in the file
name.
