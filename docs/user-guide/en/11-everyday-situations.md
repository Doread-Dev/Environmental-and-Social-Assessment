# Everyday situations

<a id="unsaved-changes"></a>
## Unsaved changes

You typed something and then try to leave the page (the left menu, a **Back** button, the
browser's back button, **Sign Out**). ESMS stops and asks **Unsaved changes**:

- **Save**: saves your changes, then goes where you were heading. If the save fails, you stay on
  the page and see why.
- **Discard changes**: leaves without saving. What you typed is lost.
- **Stay**: closes the dialog; you stay on the page with your input.

If you refresh the page or close the browser tab with unsaved changes, the browser itself asks
whether you really want to leave.

Good habit: save often. You can save as many times as you like.

<a id="someone-else-changed-this"></a>
## "Someone else changed this"

Two people opened the same page, and the other one saved first. When you then save, ESMS does not
overwrite their work. It shows **Someone else changed this**: your save was not applied.

- **Reload**: loads the latest version, with the other person's changes. What you typed on this
  page is replaced.
- **Keep editing**: closes the dialog and keeps your input on the screen. Use it to copy what you
  typed (for example into a text file), then press **Reload** and add your part again.

ESMS never mixes the two versions on its own. To avoid this, agree in the team who works on which
tool at a time.

<a id="session-expired"></a>
## Your session has expired

For safety, a sign-in lasts a limited time (12 hours unless IT changed it). If it ends while you
work, the next action shows **Your session has expired** over the page:

1. Type your **Password** (the email is already there) and press **Sign in**.
2. The dialog closes. Nothing you typed on the page is lost.
3. Press your save button again: ESMS never repeats a save on its own.

**Sign in as someone else** signs you out and opens the sign-in page; what you typed on the page
is then lost.

If the dialog says your account is deactivated, ask your administrator.

<a id="offline"></a>
## "You are offline"

An orange bar at the top says **You are offline. Some features may not work until connection is
restored.** Your computer has lost the connection to the ESMS server (the network, the Wi-Fi, or
the server is down).

- Do not close the page: what you typed stays on the screen.
- Saving does not work while you are offline. Wait until the bar disappears, then save.
- If it lasts, tell IT.

<a id="v3-buttons"></a>
## Buttons marked "Available in v3"

Some grey buttons with a lock say **Available in v3**. They are modules of the next version of
ESMS, shown so you know they are coming. They do nothing yet, for everyone.

- **Organization tools** (main menu): **GHG Emissions**, **Environmental Data Collection**.
- **Project support tools** (project menu): **Stakeholder Awareness**.
- On the SEMP overview: **SEMP Plan**.

<a id="locked-tool"></a>
## A tool is locked

A tool in grey with a lock cannot be opened yet: the tool before it is not done. The order is:
the screening approved → the assessment approved → Tools 3 and 4 complete → Tool 5. The page tells
you which step is missing and has a button to go there.

<a id="archived-notice"></a>
## The project is archived

The yellow notice **Archived project — read only** means the project was archived: you can read
it, but not change it. A reviewer can restore it.

<a id="missing-button"></a>
## A button I expected is missing

ESMS shows only the buttons your role may use, at the current step of the project. A tool that is
submitted or approved has no save button any more, and a locked tool has no buttons at all. If you
think your role is wrong, ask your administrator.

<a id="errors"></a>
## A message says something went wrong

- A page that could not load shows **Try again**. Press it; if it keeps failing, check the
  connection and tell IT.
- A save that is refused shows the reason in red (a missing field, a text that is too long, a date
  in the wrong order). Correct it and save again. Every text box takes up to 30,000 characters.
- **Page Not Found**: the address is wrong or the item was deleted. **Back to Dashboard** takes you
  home.

<a id="arabic-text"></a>
## Arabic text

You can type Arabic in every text field: project titles, locations, descriptions, the tools. The
Excel exports keep it, right to left.

<a id="theme"></a>
## Light and dark mode

The moon/sun button in the top bar (inside a project: **Dark Mode** or **Light Mode** in the menu
under your name) switches between light and dark mode. ESMS remembers your choice on this
computer.

<a id="version"></a>
## Which version am I using?

This guide describes ESMS 2.0.0. The app shows its version number in small print; IT or the
administrator may ask you for it when you report a problem.
