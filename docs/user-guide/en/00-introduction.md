# About this guide

This guide is for the people who use ESMS, the Environmental and Social Management System of the
Aga Khan Foundation – Syria. It describes ESMS version 2.0.0.

It shows the parts that match your role: you only read about what you can do in ESMS.

<a id="how-to-read"></a>
## How to read this guide

- Start with the [quick start](01-quick-start.md). It is one page.
- Then read the part you need, when you need it. Each part stands on its own.
- Words in bold (like **Save**) are exactly what you see in the app: the name of a button, a menu,
  a field or a page. Look for them on the screen.
- **Help** in the left menu opens this guide. On a tool page, the **Help** button in the top bar
  opens it at that tool. **English** and **العربية** at the top switch the language; **Print**
  prints it (or saves it as a PDF).

<a id="the-workflow"></a>
## The workflow

Every project goes through the same five tools, in this order. A tool opens only when the one
before it is done.

1. Screening (Tool 1): a first look at the project and its risk category. Submitted, then
   approved or rejected.
2. Assessment (Tool 2): the site-specific environmental assessment. Submitted, then approved or
   rejected.
3. Tools 3 and 4 (SEMP): the management activities and the mitigation plan. Saved, no approval.
4. Tool 5 (Monitoring): the baseline and four quarters of monitoring data. Saved, no approval.

Screening approved → Assessment approved → Tools 3 and 4 complete → Monitoring.

<a id="words"></a>
## Two words used in this guide

- An editor is a user who can enter and change data: Program Manager, Project Manager,
  Environmental Focal Point, Environmental Specialist and Administrator.
- A reviewer is a user who approves or rejects Tools 1 and 2: Environmental Specialist and
  Administrator.
