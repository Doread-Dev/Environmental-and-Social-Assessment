# Quick start: your first day

Do the steps below in order. Your role is shown under your name, at the top right of the screen
(or at the bottom left inside a project).

<a id="quick-start-everyone"></a>
## First, for everyone

1. Get your email address and a temporary password from your administrator.
2. Open ESMS in your browser (the address your IT department gives you) and sign in:
   **Email address**, **Password**, **Login**.
3. ESMS asks you to **Change your password**. Type the temporary password as the **Current
   password**, choose your own, and press **Save and Continue**.
4. You are on the **Dashboard**. Look at **My Latest Projects**, or open **Projects** in the left
   menu to see them all.

Details: [Signing in and finding your way](02-signing-in.md).

<a id="quick-start-users" data-requires="manage_users"></a>
## Set up the users

1. Open **Settings** (left menu, bottom) → **Users & projects**.
2. For each colleague: **Add User**, fill in **Name**, **Email**, **Role** and **Job title**, and
   press **Create User**. Copy the **Temporary password** and give it to the person yourself: it is
   shown only once.
3. Make sure there is a second administrator account, so the system is never left without one.
4. Look at the **Activity log** (in Settings) from time to time: it shows deletions, archiving and
   user changes.

Details: [Administration](10-administration.md).

<a id="quick-start-review" data-requires="approve"></a>
## Review the submitted work

1. Each day, open **Projects** and look for projects whose next step is **Pending Approval**:
   they wait for your review.
2. Open the screening or the assessment, read it, write your recommendations, then approve or
   reject it.

Details: [review a screening](05-tool-1-screening.md#screening-review),
[review an assessment](06-tool-2-assessment.md#assessment-review).

<a id="quick-start-library" data-requires="manage_library"></a>
## Fill the Library

Upload the organisation's guidance PDFs to the **Library** and choose in **Show in** on which tool
pages they appear. Details: [the Library](09-library-and-guidance.md#library-manage).

<a id="quick-start-project-work" data-requires="edit"></a>
## Start the project work

1. Create your project: **Projects** → **Create New Project**, fill in the form, **Save Project**.
2. On the project's overview, the green card tells you the next step. Press its button
   (**Open Tool** with the tool's number; the first time it opens Tool 1).
3. Fill in the screening, press **Save as Draft** as often as you like, then **Submit Screening**.
4. Wait for the review. When the screening is approved, the assessment opens; when it is rejected,
   read the reason, correct the screening and submit it again.
5. Keep the project's files in **Documents** (in the project's left menu).

Details: [Projects](04-projects.md), then the parts on each tool.

<a id="quick-start-reading" data-unless="edit"></a>
## Read the projects

1. Open **Projects** and click a project to read it.
2. Use the left menu inside the project to read each tool and the **Documents**.
3. Open the **Library** for the organisation's guidance documents.
4. Your account reads everything and changes nothing. If you need an Excel file of a project, ask
   a colleague who works on it.
