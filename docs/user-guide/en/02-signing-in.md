# Signing in and finding your way

<a id="sign-in"></a>
## Signing in

1. Open ESMS in your browser. The sign-in page shows **Environmental & Social Management System**.
2. Type your **Email address** and **Password**, then press **Login**.
   The eye icon at the end of the password field shows or hides what you typed.
3. If ESMS says your email or password is wrong, check them and try again.
4. If your account was deactivated, ESMS tells you so after a correct password. Ask your
   administrator.

**Forgot your password? Contact your system administrator.** There is no "reset by email" in ESMS
(the server works offline). Your administrator resets the password and gives you a new temporary
one.

<a id="first-password"></a>
## The first sign-in: choose your own password

A new account and a password reset both come with a temporary password, made by ESMS and given to
you by the administrator. The first time you sign in with it, ESMS shows **Change your password**
before anything else:

1. **Current password**: the temporary password.
2. **New password**: your own password. The page tells you the minimum length.
3. **Confirm new password**: the same again.
4. Press **Save and Continue**. You arrive on the Dashboard.

Until you do this, ESMS shows nothing else. **Sign out** leaves the page without changing the
password; you will be asked again next time.

<a id="the-screen"></a>
## The screen

Outside a project (Dashboard, Projects, Library):

- Left menu: **Dashboard**, **Projects**, **Library** and **Help** (this guide). Below them, under **Organization tools**,
  grey buttons marked **Available in v3** (see
  [locked buttons](11-everyday-situations.md#v3-buttons)). At the bottom, **Settings**, if your
  role has it.
- Top bar: on the right, the moon/sun button switches between dark and light mode. Your name and
  role are next to it; click them for **Profile**, **Settings** (if your role has it) and
  **Sign Out**.

Inside a project, the left menu changes to the project's own menu:

- **Back to Dashboard** at the top, then the project's name and location.
- **Project Overview**.
- **Workflow Tools**: **Screening – Tool 1**, **Assessment – Tool 2** (with **Metadata**,
  **Methods** and **Scoring** under it), **SEMP – Tools 3 & 4** and **Monitoring – Tool 5**.
  A tool that is not open yet is grey with a lock. A tool that is done shows a green tick.
- **Documents**: the project's own files.
- Under **Project support tools**, a grey **Available in v3** button.
- At the bottom: your name and role. Click them for **Profile**, **Help**, **Settings** (if your
  role has it), the light/dark switch and **Sign Out**.

The top bar inside a project shows, on the tool pages, a **Help** button that opens this guide at
that tool, and the **Guidance** button when the organisation has guidance for that tool (see
[Guidance](09-library-and-guidance.md#guidance)).

On a phone or a small tablet, the left menu is hidden. Press the menu button (three lines) at the
top left to open it.

<a id="dashboard"></a>
## The Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is the first page after signing in.

- **Total Projects**: all projects that are not archived.
- **Projects In Progress**: projects past their first step.
- **High Risk Projects**: projects whose screening put them in Category A or B.
- **Monitoring Due**: projects in the monitoring stage.
- **My Latest Projects**: the five projects changed most recently. Click one to open it.
  **View All Projects** opens the project list.

<a id="project-list"></a>
## The project list

**Projects** in the left menu lists every project.

- **Search project name or location** filters as you type.
- The first filter switches between **Active projects** and **Archived projects**.
- The status filter and the risk filter narrow the list; **Clear Filters** shows everything again.
- **Sort by:** last updated, title or start date.
- Columns: the project and its location, the **Duration**, the risk category from the screening
  (**Risk Cat.**), the **Progress (S/A/M/R)** bar (Screening, Assessment, Management plan,
  monitoring Reports), the **Status**, and **Open Project**. A click anywhere on a row opens the
  project too.

### What the project statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | the screening has not been submitted yet |
| **In Progress** | the screening is submitted or approved and the work goes on |
| **Needs Action** | the screening or the assessment was rejected and must be corrected |
| **Active Monitoring** | monitoring data is being recorded (the status filter calls it **Monitoring**) |
| **Completed** | every step is done: both reviews approved, Tools 3 and 4 complete, and at least one monitoring indicator with all four quarters filled in |
| **Archived** | the project is kept read-only |

### What the tool statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Locked** | the tool before it is not done yet |
| **Not Started** | the tool is open but empty |
| **In Progress** | work has started (a saved draft, or some rows) |
| **Submitted** (shown as **Pending Approval**) | waiting for a reviewer |
| **Approved** | accepted by a reviewer; it can no longer be changed |
| **Rejected** | sent back by a reviewer with a reason; it can be corrected and submitted again |
| **Completed** | Tools 3 and 4: every row has its description; Tool 5: at least one indicator has all four quarters filled in |

<a id="profile"></a>
## Your profile and your password

Open your name (top right) → **Profile**.

- **Account** shows your name, email, role and job title. Only an administrator can change them.
- **Change password**: type your **Current password**, a **New password** and **Confirm new
  password**, then press **Change Password**. Your other open sessions (another computer, another
  browser) stay signed in until they expire.

<a id="sign-out"></a>
## Signing out

Open your name → **Sign Out**. If the page has changes that are not saved, ESMS asks first (see
[unsaved changes](11-everyday-situations.md#unsaved-changes)).
